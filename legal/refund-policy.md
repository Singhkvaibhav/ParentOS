## The most important open question

**[REVIEW — DO NOT PUBLISH WITHOUT ADVICE]**

EU consumer law gives buyers a 14-day right of withdrawal when buying from
a *trader*. It does **not** apply to purchases from a private individual.

This marketplace is designed for parents selling their own children's
outgrown items — private individuals. But some sellers may sell often
enough, or at enough volume, to be treated as traders in law, and that
determination affects:

- whether a 14-day withdrawal right applies to their sales
- what warranty obligations they carry
- what information they must display before purchase
- what obligations fall on the platform as an intermediary

You need advice on where that line falls for your sellers, and the platform
should probably identify and label trader accounts. Until that is settled,
this policy cannot be finalised.

## When a refund is issued

**Item never received or not as described.** Either party can raise a
problem, which moves the order to `disputed` and freezes it. We review and
either refund the buyer or uphold the sale. We aim to respond within
**[FILL IN]** days.

**Listing removed by moderation.** If a listing is removed — for example a
recalled or unsafe product — any payment already taken for it is refunded
automatically, and any payment not yet captured is cancelled. This is
handled by the system, not by request.

**Change of mind.** **[REVIEW]** Whether this is a right or a courtesy
depends on the trader question above.
