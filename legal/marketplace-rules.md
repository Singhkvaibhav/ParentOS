## What this marketplace is for

Second-hand children's clothing, toys, books, equipment and accessories,
sold between families in the Helsinki region.

## Safety rules that exist for a reason

These are not generic marketplace boilerplate. They are the categories
where a used children's item can genuinely hurt a child.

**Recalled products may not be listed.** Car seats, cots, pushchairs,
slings, sleep positioners and toys are recalled regularly. Check before
listing: the EU Safety Gate portal (`ec.europa.eu/safety-gate`) and Tukes
(`tukes.fi`) publish current recalls.

**Car seats.** **[REVIEW — decide policy and state the reasoning.]** Many
marketplaces prohibit second-hand car seat sales entirely, because a seat
involved in a collision can be structurally compromised with no visible
damage, and a buyer cannot verify history. If you permit them, require
seller declaration of age, collision history and the presence of original
labels and instructions.

**Cots, mattresses and sleep products.** Safety standards change. Drop-side
cots and sleep positioners are prohibited. **[REVIEW: list current
prohibited sleep products with a specialist.]**

**Items with small detachable parts** must state the age grading they were
sold under.

**Helmets** that have been impacted may not be sold.

## Prohibited outright

- Recalled products
- Counterfeit goods
- Medicines, supplements, formula and baby food **[REVIEW]**
- Used underwear and used dummies/teats, on hygiene grounds
- Weapons and anything designed to look like one **[REVIEW]**
- Anything not primarily a children's item
- Anything the seller does not own and physically possess

## Listing honestly

Describe wear, damage, missing parts and repairs. Photograph the actual
item, not a catalogue picture. Do not list an item that is already sold
elsewhere.

## Our obligations, and the limits of them

**[REVIEW — GPSR.]** The EU General Product Safety Regulation places
obligations on online marketplaces, including cooperation with authorities,
acting on recall notices, and providing a route for safety reports. The
platform provides a "report this listing" mechanism with *safety* as the
first and most prominent reason, and moderators can remove a listing
immediately — which automatically refunds any payment already taken. Whether
this satisfies GPSR for your business needs specialist advice.

We are an intermediary, not the seller. **[REVIEW: how this is stated
matters legally and should not be overstated.]**

## Enforcement

Listings that break these rules are removed. Removal automatically cancels
or refunds any payment in progress. Repeated or serious breaches — anything
involving child safety in particular — can end an account.
