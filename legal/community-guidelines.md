## The short version

This is a marketplace used by parents. Be straightforward, be honest about
what you're selling, and turn up when you say you will.

## Expected conduct

- Reply to messages you've started
- Turn up to arranged handovers, or say you can't
- Describe items accurately, including flaws
- Leave reviews that reflect what actually happened

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory language
- Contacting someone after they've blocked you, or via another account
- Pressuring someone to complete outside the platform to avoid protections
- Requesting or sending photographs of children
- Using the platform to advertise unrelated goods or services
- Creating multiple accounts to evade a restriction or manipulate reviews

## Safety when meeting

Handovers happen in person. Meet somewhere public where practical, bring
someone if you'd rather not go alone, and check the item before completing.

## Blocking and reporting

**Blocking** is immediate and symmetric: once you block someone, neither of
you can message the other. You do not need a reason and the other person is
not told.

**Reporting** goes to moderators. Safety concerns are prioritised.

## What happens when something is reported

Reports are reviewed by a moderator, who can remove listings, restrict an
account, or close it. Removing a listing automatically refunds any payment
taken for it. Actions and reasons are recorded.

## Reviews

Review the transaction, not the person. Reviews can only be left once an
order is genuinely complete — meaning the buyer confirmed they received the
item — so a review always reflects a real, finished exchange.
